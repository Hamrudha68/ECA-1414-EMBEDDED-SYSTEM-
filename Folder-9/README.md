# Experiment 9 – Product Assembly, Inspection and Packaging using SystemC

## Aim
To simulate a product manufacturing process involving assembly, inspection and packaging using SystemC.

## Software Required
- EDA Playground
- C++ / SystemC 2.3.3

## Description
This experiment demonstrates a manufacturing process using SystemC simulation.

The process consists of three stages:
- **Assembly:** Products enter the assembly stage.
- **Inspection:** Products undergo inspection.
- **Packaging:** Products are packaged after inspection.

The simulation displays the timestamp and status of each product at different stages.

## Procedure
1. Open EDA Playground.
2. Select C++ / SystemC 2.3.3.
3. Write the SystemC program.
4. Define the production process.
5. Run the simulation.
6. Observe the execution timestamps in the output log.

## Output
The output log displays product processing stages with simulation timestamps.

Example:
- 0 ns – Product 1 entering Assembly
- 5 ns – Product 1 entering Inspection
- 8 ns – Product 1 entering Packaging
- 10 ns – Product 1 completed
- 10 ns – Product 2 entering Assembly
- 15 ns – Product 2 entering Inspection

## Result
Thus, the product manufacturing process involving assembly, inspection and packaging was successfully simulated using SystemC.
